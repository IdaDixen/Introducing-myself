About me

Who am I?

I have a background in physics and astronomy and graduated from Aarhus University a couple of years ago. During my studies I became particularly interested in programming, data and how technology can be used to solve real-world problems. Although physics and software engineering are quite different fields, I have found that many of the skills overlap. Both require logical thinking, problem solving and curiosity.

Why VIA?

I chose to study at VIA because I wanted to develop my programming skills and learn more about software development. I already had some experience with Python, SQL and data analysis, but I wanted to learn more about how software is actually designed and built. I am especially interested in learning how to create applications and websites and eventually becoming a better software developer.

My goals

One of my goals is to learn how to build complete software projects and I also want to gain practical experience by working on projects where I can use what I learn during my studies.
